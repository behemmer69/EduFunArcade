Key STEM Takeaway: The Mass-Energy Feedback Loop

Over 6 weeks, every pound gained or lost altered both your resting organ energy draw (BMR) and the mechanical work required to move your body during exercise (MET × mass × time). This natural feedback loop causes weight loss plateaus and bulking ceilings unless intake or activity is recalibrated!
