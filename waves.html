Wave Physics Insight: A ship's wake height (Amplitude A) grows with hull displacement and speed, while wave energy scales with amplitude squared (E ∝ A²). Fast speedboats generate higher-frequency (f), short-wavelength (λ) chop, whereas heavy cargo ships create long, high-amplitude rollers. When wakes from left and right ships meet at your raft, constructive interference (η = η₁ + η₂) doubles the wave slope!
